    Q1. Which letter is midway between E and K in the alphabet?
    বৰ্ণমালাত E আৰু K-ৰ মাজৰ আখৰ কোনটো?
    (A) F
    (B) G
    (C) I
    (D) H
    Answer: D) H
    Explanation: E is the 5th and K is the 11th letter of the alphabet. The midway letter is the 8th, which is H.
    বৰ্ণমালাত E হৈছে ৫ম আৰু K হৈছে ১১তম আখৰ। মাজৰ আখৰটো ৮ম অৰ্থাৎ H।

    Alphabet & Alphanumeric Series: study notes

    Read this before you attempt the questions. It is written for the DHS & DME Technical Grade III paper.

    What the alphabet series block asks

    This topic is the alphabet used as a number line. The paper gives a sequence of letters and asks which letter comes next, which letter is missing, or which term is wrong. It also asks the position of a letter from the left or the right, the letter opposite a given letter, and the alphabetical order of a set of words.

    Almost every question is solved the same way: convert the letters to numbers, find the difference between consecutive terms, and continue the pattern. The work is arithmetic on small numbers, so accuracy matters more than speed, and the paper rewards anyone who writes the numbers down instead of guessing by eye.

    The alphabet and its numbers

    Write the alphabet once with its numbers and keep that sheet in front of you for the first week. A is 1, B is 2, C is 3, D is 4, E is 5, F is 6, G is 7, H is 8, I is 9, J is 10, K is 11, L is 12, M is 13, N is 14, O is 15, P is 16, Q is 17, R is 18, S is 19, T is 20, U is 21, V is 22, W is 23, X is 24, Y is 25 and Z is 26.

    Four shortcuts save most of the time. The first is EJOTY, which gives the positions 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 for E, J, O, T and Y; from any of those you can count forward or back by one or two. The second is CFILORUX, giving 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21 and 24 for C, F, I, L, O, R, U and X. The third is that opposite letters add to 27, so A pairs with Z, B with Y, C with X, M with N, and the opposite of any letter is 27 minus its number. The fourth is that the middle of the alphabet falls between M and N, so the thirteenth letter from either end is M or N.

    The four kinds of series

    The first kind adds or subtracts a fixed number. A, C, E, G, I is plus two each time. Z, X, V, T, R is minus two. The second kind changes the difference by a fixed amount. A, B, D, G, K, P goes plus one, plus two, plus three, plus four, plus five, so the next term would be plus six. B, D, G, K, P, V goes plus two, plus three, plus four, plus five, plus six. The third kind alternates between two patterns, such as plus three then minus one, and you must find the pair by looking at four differences rather than two.

    The fourth kind is really two series written in one line. A, 1, C, 3, E, 5 alternates a letter series with a number series, so the next term is G. A, Z, C, X, F, V alternates a forward series and a reverse series, so the next is K. When a series jumps unpredictably, read every alternate term separately before looking for any other pattern.

    Worked examples

    Example one. A, D, I, P, ? The numbers are 1, 4, 9, 16, and the differences are 3, 5, 7, which rise by two, so the next difference is 9 and the answer is 25, which is Y. Notice that 1, 4, 9, 16 are also the squares of 1, 2, 3, 4, which gives the same answer faster.

    Example two. AZ, BY, CX, ? The first letters run A, B, C, so the next is D. The second letters run Z, Y, X, so the next is W. The answer is DW. Whenever a series comes in pairs, treat the first letter and the second letter as two separate series.

    Example three. A, C, F, J, O, ? The numbers are 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, and the differences are 2, 3, 4, 5, so the next difference is 6 and the answer is 21, which is U. These are the triangular numbers, and recognising them saves the addition.

    Example four. Which term is wrong: A, C, F, J, P, U? The differences are 2, 3, 4, 6, 5. The pattern should be 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, so the fifth term should be 15, which is O, and P is the wrong term. In a wrong term question, check whether one correction makes the whole series regular - if it does, that term is the answer.

    Position questions and alphabetical order

    A position question is answered with the arithmetic of 27. The fifteenth letter from the left is O. The fifth letter from the right is 27 minus 5, which is 22, so it is V. The letter opposite M is 27 minus 13, which is 14, so it is N. If a question asks how many letters lie between two given letters, subtract their positions and then subtract one, because the two end letters are not counted.

    Alphabetical order questions need patience, not tricks. Compare words letter by letter and stop at the first place where they differ. In Nation, Native, Nasty and Naval, the first difference appears at the third letter, where s comes before t and t before v, so Nasty comes first; then Nation and Native differ at the fifth letter, where o comes before v. The order is Nasty, Nation, Native, Naval.

    Word formation questions ask whether a given word can be made from the letters of a longer word. Count the letters of the longer word, then check that the smaller word does not use a letter more times than it appears. For example, from TRANSPORT you can form PART, PORT, SPORT and TRAIN, but not STRAP twice over if the letters run out. The counting step is what the question is testing.

    Where marks are lost

    Mixed series, and a short practice set

    A mixed series combines letters, numbers and sometimes symbols, and it is solved by separating the components. In 2A, 4C, 6E, 8G the numbers rise by two and the letters rise by two, so the next term is 10I. In A2Z, C4X, E6V the numbers rise by two, the first letters rise by two and the last letters fall by two, so the next is G8T. Once the parts are separated, each part is an ordinary series.

    Practise these five aloud, writing the numbers underneath. First, D, F, H, J, ? - the answer is L. Second, Z, W, T, Q, ? - the answer is N, since the difference is minus three. Third, B, E, I, N, ? - the differences are 3, 4, 5, so the next is plus six and the answer is T. Fourth, AB, DE, GH, ? - the answer is JK. Fifth, which is wrong: A, B, D, H, P - the differences are 1, 2, 4, 8, and the correct series doubles, so all four terms are right and the question would ask for the next, which is 16 plus 15, giving 31, beyond the alphabet.

    A useful check before answering is to see whether the answer must lie inside the alphabet. If a pattern runs past Z, the paper usually stops the series before that point, and an option beyond Z is a signal that the pattern has been misread.

    Three errors recur. A series is continued with the last difference instead of the pattern of differences. Alternate-term series are read as a single series, so the answer comes out half a step wrong. And in a between question the two end letters are counted, which makes the answer one too high. Write the numbers under the letters and the errors disappear.

    • Convert letters to numbers first; never guess the next letter by eye.
    • EJOTY gives 5, 10, 15, 20, 25; CFILORUX gives 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24.
    • Opposite letters add to 27; between two letters is the difference minus one.
    • If a series jumps, split it into alternate terms and solve each one.
    • In a wrong term question, test whether one correction makes the whole series regular.
    Exam tip: For any letter series, write the numbers 1 to 26 under the letters before doing anything else. The pattern is always visible in the differences, and it is invisible in the letters.

    মিশ্ৰ শৃংখলা, আৰু চুটি অভ্যাস

    মিশ্ৰ শৃংখলাত আখৰ, সংখ্যা আৰু কেতিয়াবা চিহ্ন মিলি আহে, আৰু অংশবোৰ পৃথক কৰি সমাধান কৰা হয়। 2A, 4C, 6E, 8G ত সংখ্যা দুগুণে বাঢ়ে আৰু আখৰ দুগুণে বাঢ়ে, সেয়ে পৰৱৰ্তী পদ 10I। A2Z, C4X, E6V ত সংখ্যা দুগুণে বাঢ়ে, প্ৰথম আখৰ দুগুণে বাঢ়ে আৰু শেষ আখৰ দুগুণে কমে, সেয়ে পৰৱৰ্তী G8T। অংশবোৰ পৃথক কৰিলে প্ৰতিটো অংশ সাধাৰণ শৃংখলা হয়।

    এই পাঁচটা মুখেৰে অভ্যাস কৰা, তলত সংখ্যা লিখি। প্ৰথম, D, F, H, J, ? - উত্তৰ L। দ্বিতীয়, Z, W, T, Q, ? - উত্তৰ N, কাৰণ পাৰ্থক্য তিনি বিয়োগ। তৃতীয়, B, E, I, N, ? - পাৰ্থক্য 3, 4, 5, সেয়ে পৰৱৰ্তী ছয় যোগ আৰু উত্তৰ T। চতুৰ্থ, AB, DE, GH, ? - উত্তৰ JK। পঞ্চম, কোনটো ভুল: A, B, D, H, P - পাৰ্থক্য 1, 2, 4, 8, আৰু শুদ্ধ শৃংখলা দুগুণে বাঢ়ে, সেয়ে চাৰিওটা পদ শুদ্ধ আৰু প্ৰশ্নই পৰৱৰ্তী পদ সোধে।

    উত্তৰ দিয়াৰ আগতে এটা সহায়ক পৰীক্ষা: উত্তৰ বৰ্ণমালাৰ ভিতৰত থাকিব লাগিব নে নাই চোৱা। আৰ্হি Z ৰ সিপাৰলৈ গ'লে প্ৰশ্নকাকতে সাধাৰণতে তাতকৈ আগতে শৃংখলা ৰখায়, আৰু Z ৰ বাহিৰৰ বিকল্পই আৰ্হি ভুল পঢ়াৰ সংকেত দিয়ে।

    আখৰৰ স্থান লিখাৰ অভ্যাস

    প্ৰতিদিনে দহ মিনিট বৰ্ণমালাৰ স্থান লিখাৰ অভ্যাস কৰা। প্ৰথমে 1 ৰ পৰা 26 লৈকে পোনকৈ, তাৰ পিছত 26 ৰ পৰা 1 লৈকে ওলোটাকৈ, তাৰ পিছত EJOTY আৰু CFILORUX ৰ পৰা আগলৈ-পিছলৈ। এই অভ্যাসৰ পিছত প্ৰশ্নত সংখ্যা লিখিবলৈ সময় নালাগে, আৰু মনটোৱে আৰ্হিতহে কাম কৰে।

    তিনিটা ভুল বাৰে বাৰে আহে। শৃংখলাক পাৰ্থক্যৰ আৰ্হিৰ সলনি শেষ পাৰ্থক্যৰে আগবঢ়াই নিয়া হয়। পাল পাতি অহা শৃংখলাক একেই শৃংখলা হিচাপে পঢ়া হয়, সেয়ে উত্তৰ আধা স্তৰ ভুল হয়। আৰু মাজত কিমানৰ প্ৰশ্নত দুই মূৰৰ আখৰ গণনা কৰা হয়, যাৰ ফলত উত্তৰ এটা বেছি হয়। আখৰৰ তলত সংখ্যা লিখিলে এই ভুলবোৰ নোহোৱা হয়।

    • আগতে আখৰক সংখ্যালৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰা; চকুৰে পৰৱৰ্তী আখৰ কেতিয়াও আন্দাজ নকৰিবা।
    • EJOTY য়ে 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 দিয়ে; CFILORUX য়ে 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24 দিয়ে।
    • বিপৰীত আখৰৰ যোগফল 27; দুটা আখৰৰ মাজত হ'ল পাৰ্থক্য বিয়োগ এক।
    • শৃংখলা জাঁপ দিলে পাল পাতি পদত ভাঙি প্ৰতিটো আলাদাকৈ সমাধান কৰা।
    • ভুল পদৰ প্ৰশ্নত চোৱা, এটা শুধৰণিয়ে গোটেই শৃংখলা নিয়মীয়া কৰে নে নাই।

    শৃংখলাৰ সাধাৰণ আৰ্হিবোৰ

    প্ৰশ্নকাকতত অহা আৰ্হিবোৰ চিনি পোৱাটোৱেই আধা কাম। সাধাৰণ আৰ্হি হ'ল: স্থিৰ যোগ বা বিয়োগ, বাঢ়ি যোৱা যোগ, কমি যোৱা যোগ, বৰ্গ, ঘন, মৌলিক সংখ্যা, দুগুণ, আৰু পাল পাতি দুটা শৃংখলা। প্ৰতিটো আৰ্হিৰ এটা চিনাকি ৰূপ মনত ৰাখিলে নতুন প্ৰশ্ন চিনাকি যেন লাগে।

    মৌলিক সংখ্যাৰ আৰ্হি এটা উদাহৰণ: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11 - আখৰত B, C, E, G, K। বৰ্গৰ আৰ্হি: 1, 4, 9, 16 - A, D, I, P। ঘনৰ আৰ্হি: 1, 8, 27 - A, H ৰ পিছত বৰ্ণমালাৰ সীমাৰ বাহিৰলৈ যায়, সেয়ে ঘনৰ প্ৰশ্ন সাধাৰণতে সংখ্যাৰ শৃংখলাতহে আহে।

    সময় কেনেকৈ বচাব

    প্ৰথমে সংখ্যা লিখা, তাৰ পিছত পাৰ্থক্য লিখা, তাৰ পিছত আৰ্হি চিনা - এই তিনি স্তৰত প্ৰতিটো প্ৰশ্ন চলাব। প্ৰথম দুটা পাৰ্থক্যই আৰ্হি নোকোৱালে পাল পাতি পদ চোৱা। এই ক্ৰমটো মূৰত ৰাখিলে প্ৰশ্ন এটাত এখন-দেৰ মিনিটতকৈ বেছি নালাগে।

    পৰীক্ষাৰ হলত এখন ৰাফ শ্বীটত 1 ৰ পৰা 26 লৈকে বৰ্ণমালা লিখি থোৱা ভাল। সেয়া প্ৰতিবাৰ লিখাৰ পৰা ৰক্ষা কৰে আৰু মনত পেলোৱাৰ ভুলো কমায়।

    পৰীক্ষাৰ টিপ: আখৰৰ যিকোনো শৃংখলাত আন একো নকৰি আগতে আখৰবোৰৰ তলত 1 ৰ পৰা 26 লৈকে সংখ্যা লিখা। আৰ্হি সদায় পাৰ্থক্যত দেখা যায়, আখৰত কেতিয়াও নেদেখা।
