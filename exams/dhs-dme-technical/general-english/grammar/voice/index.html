    Active & Passive Voice

    Q1. Change into passive: 'Open the door.'
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ সলাওক: 'Open the door.'
    (A) The door is opened.
    (B) Let the door be opened.
    (C) The door was opened.
    (D) The door has been opened.
    Answer: B) Let the door be opened.
    Explanation: An imperative sentence becomes 'Let ... be + past participle' in the passive.
    imperative বাক্য passive ত 'Let ... be + past participle' লৈ পৰিণত হয়।
    Q2. Change into passive: 'Who wrote this book?'
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ সলাওক: 'Who wrote this book?'
    (A) By whom was this book written?
    (B) Who was this book written?
    (C) By who this book was written?
    (D) This book wrote by whom?
    Answer: A) By whom was this book written?
    Explanation: The passive of 'Who wrote this book?' is 'By whom was this book written?'.
    'Who wrote this book?' ৰ passive হৈছে 'By whom was this book written?'।
    Q3. Change into passive voice: They built the bridge in 1990.
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: They built the bridge in 1990.
    (A) The bridge was built in 1990.
    (B) The bridge is built in 1990.
    (C) The bridge has built in 1990.
    (D) The bridge built in 1990.
    Answer: A) The bridge was built in 1990.
    Explanation: The simple past active 'built' becomes 'was built' in the passive voice.
    simple past ৰ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য 'built' কৰ্মবাচ্যত 'was built' হয়।
    Q4. Change into active voice: The letter was written by her.
    কৰ্তৃবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: The letter was written by her.
    (A) She wrote the letter.
    (B) She writes the letter.
    (C) She has wrote the letter.
    (D) The letter wrote her.
    Answer: A) She wrote the letter.
    Explanation: In the active voice the subject performs the action: 'She wrote the letter.'
    কৰ্তৃবাচ্যত কৰ্তাই কাৰ্য কৰে: 'She wrote the letter.'
