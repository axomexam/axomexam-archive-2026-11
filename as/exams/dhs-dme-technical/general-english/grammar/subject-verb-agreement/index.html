    Q1. Neither Ram ___ Shyam was present.
    (A) or
    (B) and
    (C) nor
    (D) but
    Answer: C) nor
    'neither ... nor' হৈছে শুদ্ধ যুগল।
    Explanation: 'Neither ... nor' is the correct correlative pair.

    (A) are
    (B) is
    (C) am
    (D) were
    Answer: C) am
    'either ... or' ৰ সৈতে verb ওচৰৰ subject 'I' ৰ লগত মিলে, সেয়ে 'am' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: With 'either ... or', the verb agrees with the nearer subject 'I', so 'am' is used.
