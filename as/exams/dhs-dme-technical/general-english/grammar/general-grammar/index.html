    সাধাৰণ ব্যাকৰণ (মিশ্ৰ অনুশীলন) • General English • DHS & DME Technical Grade III

    35 Questions with Answers

    কঠোৰ পৰিশ্ৰম কৰা, নহ'লে তুমি অনুত্তীৰ্ণ হ'বা।
    Q1. Work hard ___ you should fail.
    (A) lest
    (B) unless
    (C) until
    (D) if
    Answer: A) lest
    'lest' ৰ পিছত 'should' আহে আৰু ই 'ভয়ত যাতে' বুজায়।
    Explanation: 'Lest' is followed by 'should' and means 'for fear that'.
    তেওঁ কেৱল গান নকৰে, নৃত্যও কৰে।
    Q2. He not only sings ___ dances.
    (A) and
    (B) but also
    (C) or
    (D) nor
    Answer: B) but also
    'not only ... but also' হৈছে শুদ্ধ correlative যুগল।
    Explanation: 'Not only ... but also' is the correct correlative pair.
    তেওঁ ইমান ভাগৰুৱা যে খোজ কাঢ়িব নোৱাৰে।
    Q3. He is so tired ___ he cannot walk.
    (A) so
    (B) as
    (C) that
    (D) than
    Answer: C) that
    'so ... that' হৈছে শুদ্ধ correlative যুগল।
    Explanation: 'So ... that' is the correct correlative pair.
    ভুল উলিয়াওক: 'He is senior than me.'
    Q4. Find the error: 'He is senior than me.'
    (A) He is
    (B) senior
    (C) than
    (D) me
    Answer: C) than
    'senior' ৰ পিছত 'than' নহয়, 'to' আহে।
    Explanation: 'Senior' is followed by 'to', not 'than'.
    ভুল উলিয়াওক: 'One of my friend is a doctor.'
    Q5. Find the error: 'One of my friend is a doctor.'
    (A) One of
    (B) friend
    (C) is
    (D) a doctor
    Answer: B) friend
    'one of' ৰ পিছত plural noun আহে, সেয়ে 'friends' শুদ্ধ।
    Explanation: 'One of' is followed by a plural noun, so 'friends' is correct.
    যিজনে নিজৰ দেশক ভাল পায় তেওঁক কি বোলে?
    Q6. 'A person who loves his country' is called a:
    (A) Patriot
    (B) Traitor
    (C) Martyr
    (D) Refugee
    Answer: A) Patriot
    নিজৰ দেশক ভালপোৱা ব্যক্তিক patriot বোলা হয়।
    Explanation: A person who loves his country is called a patriot.
    যিজনে পঢ়িব-লিখিব নোৱাৰে তেওঁক কি বোলে?
    Q7. 'A person who cannot read or write' is called an:
    (A) Illegible
    (B) Illiterate
    (C) Ignorant
    (D) Innocent
    Answer: B) Illiterate
    পঢ়িব বা লিখিব নজনা ব্যক্তিক illiterate বোলা হয়।
    Explanation: A person who cannot read or write is called illiterate.
    চৰাই ৰখা স্থানক কি বোলে?
    Q8. 'A place where birds are kept' is called an:
    (A) Aquarium
    (B) Apiary
    (C) Aviary
    (D) Arena
    Answer: C) Aviary
    চৰাই ৰখা ঠাইক aviary বোলা হয়।
    Explanation: A place where birds are kept is called an aviary.
    যি সৰ্বত্ৰ উপস্থিত তেওঁক কি বোলে?
    Q9. 'One who is present everywhere' is called:
    (A) Omniscient
    (B) Omnipotent
    (C) Omnivorous
    (D) Omnipresent
    Answer: D) Omnipresent
    সৰ্বত্ৰ উপস্থিত থকা জনক omnipresent বোলা হয়।
    Explanation: One who is present everywhere is called omnipresent.
    যি হস্তলিপি পঢ়িব নোৱাৰি তাক কি বোলে?
    Q10. 'A handwriting that cannot be read' is:
    (A) Illegible
    (B) Illegal
    (C) Illiterate
    (D) Ineligible
    Answer: A) Illegible
    পঢ়িব নোৱাৰা হস্তলিখনক illegible বোলা হয়।
    Explanation: A handwriting that cannot be read is called illegible.
    'brave' ৰ সমাৰ্থক শব্দ বাছক:
    Q11. Choose the synonym of 'brave':
    (A) coward
    (B) timid
    (C) courageous
    (D) weak
    Answer: C) courageous
    'courageous' হৈছে 'brave' ৰ সমাৰ্থক শব্দ।
    Explanation: 'Courageous' is a synonym of 'brave'.
    'ancient' ৰ বিপৰীত শব্দ বাছক:
    Q12. Choose the antonym of 'ancient':
    (A) old
    (B) modern
    (C) historic
    (D) past
    Answer: B) modern
    'modern' হৈছে 'ancient' ৰ বিপৰীত শব্দ।
    Explanation: 'Modern' is the antonym of 'ancient'.
    তেওঁ ওলাই যোৱাৰ লগে লগে এজন অতিথি আহিল।
    Q13. Scarcely had he gone out ___ a guest arrived.
    (A) than
    (B) when
    (C) then
    (D) that
    Answer: B) when
    'scarcely ... when' হৈছে শুদ্ধ correlative যুগল।
    Explanation: 'Scarcely ... when' is the correct correlative pair.
    তেওঁ মোক ক'লে, 'Please help me.' পৰোক্ষ উক্তি কি?
    Q14. He said to me, 'Please help me.' The indirect form is:
    (A) He told me please help him.
    (B) He requested me to help him.
    (C) He said me to help him.
    (D) He asked that I help him.
    Answer: B) He requested me to help him.
    নম্ৰ অনুৰোধ indirect speech ত 'requested ... to' লৈ পৰিণত হয়।
    Explanation: A polite request becomes 'requested ... to' in indirect speech.
    'begin' ৰ সমাৰ্থক শব্দ বাছক:
    Q15. Choose the synonym of 'begin':
    (A) end
    (B) finish
    (C) commence
    (D) stop
    Answer: C) commence
    'commence' হৈছে 'begin' ৰ সমাৰ্থক শব্দ।
    Explanation: 'Commence' is a synonym of 'begin'.
    বহু ভাষা কোৱা ব্যক্তিক কি বোলে?
    Q16. 'A person who speaks many languages' is called a:
    (A) Linguist
    (B) Polyglot
    (C) Orator
    (D) Interpreter
    Answer: B) Polyglot
    বহু ভাষা কোৱা ব্যক্তিক polyglot বোলা হয়।
    Explanation: A person who speaks many languages is called a polyglot.
    যিজনে বিষয়ৰ উজ্জ্বল দিশ চাই তেওঁক কি বোলে?
    Q17. 'One who looks at the bright side of things' is an:
    (A) Pessimist
    (B) Optimist
    (C) Atheist
    (D) Theist
    Answer: B) Optimist
    বস্তুৰ উজ্জ্বল দিশ চোৱা ব্যক্তিক optimist বোলা হয়।
    Explanation: One who looks at the bright side of things is an optimist.
    'to hit the nail on the head' ৰ অৰ্থ কি?
    Q18. The idiom 'to hit the nail on the head' means:
    (A) To hurt someone কাকোবা আঘাত কৰা
    (B) To do the right thing exactly ঠিক কথাটো কৰা
    (C) To work with a hammer ঘাণেৰে কাম কৰা
    (D) To make a mistake ভুল কৰা
    B) ঠিক কথাটো কৰা
    Answer: B) To do the right thing exactly
    'to hit the nail on the head' মানে একেবাৰে শুদ্ধ কাম কৰা।
    Explanation: 'To hit the nail on the head' means to do the right thing exactly.
    'duke' ৰ স্ত্ৰীলিংগ কি?
    Q19. The feminine of 'duke' is:
    (A) dukes
    (B) duchess
    (C) dukeess
    (D) lady duke
    Answer: B) duchess
    'duke' ৰ স্ত্ৰীলিংগ হৈছে 'duchess'।
    Explanation: The feminine of 'duke' is 'duchess'.
    'Shut the door' বাক্যটো কি প্ৰকাৰৰ?
    Q20. The sentence 'Shut the door' is:
    (A) Assertive নিৰ্দেশাত্মক
    (B) Imperative আদেশাত্মক
    (C) Exclamatory বিস্ময়সূচক
    (D) Optative ইচ্ছাসূচক
    B) আদেশাত্মক
    Answer: B) Imperative
    'Shut the door' য়ে আদেশ দিয়ে, সেয়ে ই imperative।
    Explanation: 'Shut the door' gives a command, so it is imperative.
    তেওঁ আৰু তেওঁৰ বন্ধুসকলে আহি আছে।
    Q21. He as well as his friends ___ coming.
    (A) are
    (B) were
    (C) is
    (D) have
    Answer: C) is
    'as well as' ৰ সৈতে verb প্ৰথম subject 'he' ৰ লগত মিলে।
    Explanation: With 'as well as', the verb agrees with the first subject 'he'.
    'arrival' ৰ বিপৰীত শব্দ কি?
    Q22. The opposite of 'arrival' is:
    (A) coming
    (B) departure
    (C) reach
    (D) entry
    Answer: B) departure
    'departure' হৈছে 'arrival' ৰ বিপৰীত শব্দ।
    Explanation: 'Departure' is the opposite of 'arrival'.
    খালী ঠাই পূৰ কৰক: 'He is ___ M.A. in English.'
    Q23. Fill in the blank: 'He is ___ M.A. in English.'
    (A) a
    (B) an
    (C) the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: B) an
    'M.A.' ৰ উচ্চাৰণ 'em' স্বৰ ধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়, সেয়ে 'an' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'M.A.' begins with the vowel sound 'em', so 'an' is used.
    তাই ইংৰাজীত ___ এম.এ.।
    Q24. She is ___ M.A. in English.
    (A) a
    (B) an
    (C) the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: B) an
    M আখৰৰ উচ্চাৰণ 'em', যি স্বৰধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়; সেয়ে 'an' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: The letter M is pronounced 'em', which begins with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used.
    দুজন ল'ৰাৰ ___ শাস্তি পাইছিল।
    Q25. ___ of the two boys was punished.
    (A) Both
    (B) All
    (C) Either
    (D) Neither
    Answer: D) Neither
    'Neither' মানে দুজনৰ এজনো নহয়; ই singular verb লয়; সেয়ে 'Neither of the two boys was punished' শুদ্ধ।
    Explanation: 'Neither' means not one of the two; it takes a singular verb, so 'Neither of the two boys was punished' is correct.
    তাই ভুলটোৰ বাবে নিজকে দোষ দিলে।
    Q26. She blamed ___ for the mistake.
    (A) her
    (B) hers
    (C) herself
    (D) she
    Answer: C) herself
    কৰ্তা আৰু কৰ্ম একেই ব্যক্তি হ'লে reflexive pronoun 'herself' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'Herself' is the reflexive pronoun used when the subject and object are the same person.
    ___ সূৰ্য পূব দিশে উদয় হয়।
    Q27. ___ Sun rises in the east.
    (A) A
    (B) An
    (C) The
    (D) No article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: C) The
    সূৰ্যৰ দৰে একক বস্তুৰ আগত 'The' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'The' is used before unique objects like the sun.
    তেওঁ অসুস্থ আছিল, ___ তেওঁ শ্ৰেণীত উপস্থিত থাকিল।
    Q28. He was ill, ___ he attended the class.
    (A) and
    (B) or
    (C) so
    (D) but
    Answer: D) but
    অসুস্থ হোৱা আৰু শ্ৰেণীত উপস্থিত থকাৰ মাজৰ বিপৰীত ভাৱ দেখুৱাবলৈ 'but' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'But' shows contrast between being ill and attending the class.
    তেওঁ যোৱা বছৰ দিল্লীলৈ ___।
    Q29. He ___ to Delhi last year.
    (A) go
    (B) goes
    (C) went
    (D) has gone
    Answer: C) went
    'last year' এ অতীত সময় বুজায়; সেয়ে simple past 'went' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'Last year' refers to past time, so the simple past 'went' is used.
    তোমালোকে তোমালোকৰ পিতৃ-মাতৃক সন্মান কৰা ___।
    Q30. You ___ respect your parents.
    (A) should
    (B) may
    (C) can
    (D) would
    Answer: A) should
    'should' য়ে নৈতিক কৰ্তব্য বা উপদেশ বুজায়।
    Explanation: 'Should' expresses moral duty or advice.
    তাই আহি আছে, ___?
    Q31. She is coming, ___?
    (A) is she
    (B) isn't she
    (C) does she
    (D) doesn't she
    Answer: B) isn't she
    ধনাত্মক বাক্যৰ সৈতে ঋণাত্মক question tag লগে; auxiliary 'is', সেয়ে tag 'isn't she'।
    Explanation: A positive statement takes a negative question tag; the auxiliary is 'is', so the tag is 'isn't she'.
    যদি বৰষুণ দিয়ে, আমি খেলখন ___।
    Q32. If it rains, we ___ the match.
    (A) cancel
    (B) cancelled
    (C) will cancel
    (D) would cancel
    Answer: C) will cancel
    first conditional বাক্যত main clause ত 'will' আৰু verb ৰ base form ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: In a first conditional sentence, the main clause uses 'will' plus the base verb.
    সৰল বৰ্তমান কালৰ শুদ্ধ ঋণাত্মক বাক্যটো বাছক:
    Q33. Choose the correct negative sentence in simple present:
    (A) He don't like tea.
    (B) He not like tea.
    (C) He didn't likes tea.
    (D) He doesn't like tea.
    Answer: D) He doesn't like tea.
    'he' ৰ সৈতে present simple ৰ ঋণাত্মক ৰূপ 'doesn't' আৰু verb ৰ base form ৰে গঠন হয়।
    Explanation: In the present simple with 'he', the negative is formed with 'doesn't' and the base verb.
    আমি চিৰিয়াখানাত ___ হাতী দেখিলোঁ।
    Q34. We saw ___ elephant in the zoo.
    (A) a
    (B) an
    (C) the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: B) an
    'elephant' ৰ উচ্চাৰণ স্বৰধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়; সেয়ে 'an' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'Elephant' begins with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used.
    তেওঁ শ্ৰেণীৰ ___ শ্ৰেষ্ঠ ছাত্ৰ।
    Q35. He is ___ best student in the class.
    (A) a
    (B) an
    (C) the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: C) the
    superlative degree ৰ আগত 'the' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'The' is used before a superlative degree.
