    A sample of the grammar questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. He is ___ honest man. তেওঁ ___ সৎ মানুহ।
      (A) a   (B) an   (C) the   (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
      Answer: an Explanation: 'Honest' begins with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used.
    • Q2. ___ Ganga is a holy river. ___ গংগা এখন পবিত্ৰ নদী।
      (A) A   (B) An   (C) The   (D) No article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
      Answer: The Explanation: 'The' is used before the names of rivers.
    • Q3. She bought ___ unique painting. তাই ___ অনন্য চিত্ৰ কিনিলে।
      (A) a   (B) an   (C) the   (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
      Answer: a Explanation: 'Unique' begins with the consonant sound 'yu', so 'a' is used.
    • Q4. The plural of 'child' is: 'child' ৰ বহুবচন কি?
      (A) childs   (B) childes   (C) children   (D) childrens
      Answer: children Explanation: The plural of 'child' is 'children'.

    How to use the grammar mock test

    • Attempt a set under the timer without pausing, to copy exam conditions.
    • Read the explanation for every question you got wrong immediately after the set.
    • Note the topic behind each mistake and revise that topic before the next set.
    • Re-attempt the same set after a week and compare your score.
